RELAY SUPPORT DECISION POLICY

RESOLVE
Use RESOLVE only when:
- The answer is explicitly supported by available policy or product context.
- The request is a standard documented procedure.
- No sensitive account change or human approval is required.
- Password reset instructions may be resolved directly.

REQUEST_INFO
Use REQUEST_INFO when:
- Required account or billing information is missing.
- The request requires information that is not available in the provided context.
- A product capability cannot be verified.
- Current service or outage status cannot be verified.
- A billing request cannot be completed without identifying information.
- A subscription change lacks required account context.

ESCALATE
Use ESCALATE when:
- There is suspected unauthorized access or a security incident.
- Confidential customer information may have been accessed.
- An account or user was unexpectedly deleted, locked, or compromised.
- The request involves sensitive permissions or access verification.
- A privacy or permanent data-deletion request is made.
- SSO configuration requires administrator intervention.
- A refund exceeds $500.
- A technical problem requires intervention beyond documented troubleshooting.

CORE SAFETY RULE

Never assume that a product feature, account state, service status,
permission, or company policy is true when it is not explicitly available
in the provided context.
